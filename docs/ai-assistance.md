# AI assistance record for the denoiser study

Prepared 27 November 2026. This record describes assistance in the current Codex
conversation. It is a draft for the team to review against the course's actual
submission requirements. It does not assert authorship for the initial codebase
or replace the original proposal's citations.

## Suggested disclosure

OpenAI Codex assisted with diagnosing and implementing rendering/evaluation
controls, preparing and checking experiments, analyzing recorded data, producing
figures and drafting the denoiser report, presentation and handoff documentation.
Assisted areas include initial object-motion history, GPU timestamp association,
fixed temporal input and DLSS K exposure controls, local evaluation/verification
scripts, real-window recording/checks and presentation profiles. AI also helped
draft explanations and locate primary literature. The team remains responsible
for reviewing the code, the experimental interpretation and the submitted work.

The scripts retain commands, binaries/input hashes, frame state, timestamps and
verification results. Reported scientific conclusions are tied to those records.
No failed fixture is presented as passing and no numerical acceptance bound was
relaxed to hide a failure. The ordinary Performance-M color fixture remains a
known unresolved failure; the study explicitly selects K where ordinary DLSS is
used. The GPU suite is not described as fully passing.
